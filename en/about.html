Why Combat Coach exists

Solo training in fight sports rarely fails on conditioning — it fails on the double role. Standing alone at the bag, you have to be coach and athlete at once: inventing combinations, watching the clock, managing intensity. The result is predictable and has little to do with a real fight.

Combat Coach takes the coaching role off your shoulders. The ComboSim timer calls out combinations, runs the rounds and sets the pace. You only have to do one thing: react and strike — exactly what was missing from training alone at the bag, before the app existed.

How the content on this site is made

The guides here are not filler written for search engines. They follow the same principles as the app:

  • One source for technique and structure — combinations, round formats and drill order match the training logic built into Combat Coach.
  • Traceable structure — every drill is named, placed in context and given a concrete purpose, rather than listed loosely.
  • Visible update date — every page states when it was last revised.
  • Corrections welcome — spotted a mistake? Write to info@combatcoach.org. Feedback from the gym floor is explicitly wanted.
